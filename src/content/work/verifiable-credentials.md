## The question

How could signing move beyond a mark on a document toward stronger, reusable evidence of who someone is and what they are authorised to do?

## What was explored

- Signing with verifiable credentials.
- Credential issuance.
- A digital wallet completing the issuer-holder-verifier trust triangle.

## Why this remains a field note

This was an emerging product-line pilot, so the most useful public version should focus on the framing, assumptions, evidence and unresolved questions - not present exploration as a finished commercial outcome.
