## The opportunity

A partner needed signing capabilities embedded into its workflow. That required more than exposing endpoints: the product, technical and commercial experience had to work as one system.

## My contribution

- Led product delivery for the first public integration.
- Worked across customer need, platform feasibility and partnership outcomes.
- Helped translate the integration into a repeatable product direction rather than a one-off build.

The integration helped secure the Renti partnership, expand market presence and open a new revenue path. The complete case study should add the decision sequence and implementation constraints once those details are cleared for publication.
