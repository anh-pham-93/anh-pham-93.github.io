## The idea

This is not intended to replace Goodreads or become a log of everything I finish. It is a selective shelf for books that stayed with me and that I would recommend for a specific reason.

## Suggested review structure

- What the book is about.
- Why I picked it up.
- What stayed with me.
- Who I would recommend it to.
